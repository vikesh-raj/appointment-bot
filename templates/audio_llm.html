How it works
  1. Click "Start Recording" and speak your appointment details
  2. Include your name, phone number, preferred date and time, and service type
  3. Click "Stop Recording" when done
  4. The AI will process your request and create an appointment

Example: "Hi, I'd like to book an appointment. My name is John Doe, my number is 555-123-4567. I need a haircut on December 15th, 2025 at 2:00 PM."
